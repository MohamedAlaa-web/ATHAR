# ATHAR — Deviations Log

Running log for the indoor-navigation prototype. Every entry says **what** changed
from the spec, **why**, **severity** (low / medium / high) and **impact** on the demo
or on honesty. Entries are dated 2026-10-23 unless noted. Entries D-25 onward are
from 2026-10-24 (walker modes, self-hosted fonts, PNG icons, Device check, deployment prep,
debug stale badge, QR-reset reporting, printable QR sheet, demo build, live hosting).

Legend: severity is the risk to the demo or to a truthful claim, not the effort.

## Explicit spec options we chose (not deviations)

- **Kalman filter**, not a particle filter — the spec allows either. A constant-velocity
  Kalman filter with per-source model noise and map-matching projection measured
  1.73 m mean error at low crowd, so a particle filter was not justified.
- **Robust weighted least squares trilateration** over the strongest 4 beacons, with
  inverse-variance weights and residual trimming.
- **jsQR** at ~3.8 frames/s while the scan sheet is open, instead of `BarcodeDetector`
  (not available on iOS Safari); the camera stream stops on decode or on sheet close.
- **SVG floor plan + React state updates at 1–3 Hz** instead of a canvas/60 fps loop.
- **Arabic default with an instant English toggle**; French remains only on legacy pages.
- **BLE left as a documented stub** (`BleSource` + `NativeBleBridge`); it reports
  `native-only` and never fabricates a position.
